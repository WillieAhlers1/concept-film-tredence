# The Tredence voice (references/narration.md): start in the customer's world; the last mile (the everyday decision
# that changes); "we" and "you" together; calm, specific, confident, never promotional; no exclamation marks.

# Close: how the business could work and what it could be worth, then one ask, spoken as the ask appears on screen.
